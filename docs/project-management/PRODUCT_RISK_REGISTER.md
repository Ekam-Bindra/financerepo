# Product Risk Register

Task: `PRG-006`

Scoring and governance follow `RISK_MANAGEMENT.md`. All entries are initial
planning risks. “Target” is the maximum intended residual score after the
listed controls are verified.

| ID | Risk statement and consequence | L | I | Score | Target | Accountable owner | Preventive controls and mitigation | Trigger / early warning | Contingency | Status / next review |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| PR-005 | Source licensing, usage, copyright, redistribution, transcript, audio, or AI-provider terms prohibit an intended workflow. | 3 | 5 | 15 | 5 | Compliance and Legal Reviewer | Source inventory; rights classification; legal approval before enablement; source-level policy enforcement; audit trail. | A source lacks approved rights or terms change after approval. | Disable the source, preserve required audit records, notify impacted users, and switch to an approved alternative. | Identified / `PRG-008`–`PRG-010` |

| PR-009 | Report and dashboard complexity overwhelms users or hides source evidence, reducing usability and increasing interpretation errors. | 3 | 4 | 12 | 6 | UX and Accessibility Designer | Workflow-centered information architecture; configurable modules; accessible interaction patterns; staged usability testing. | Task-completion, error, or abandonment measures miss approved usability thresholds. | Simplify default views, expose evidence before advanced analysis, and defer low-value modules. | Identified / Phase 10 beta |

| PR-011 | Slow responses, long report generation, or delayed filing availability cause abandonment despite functional correctness. | 3 | 4 | 12 | 6 | Enterprise Product Manager | Published performance targets; async progress states; incremental results; latency telemetry; capacity testing. | Dashboard exceeds 3 seconds p95, common Q&A exceeds 15 seconds p95, or reports exceed five minutes. | Degrade to cached/source-only views, queue long work, and communicate recovery expectations. | Identified / performance gates |
| PR-012 | AI or infrastructure cost makes the product economically unsustainable relative to analyst value. | 3 | 4 | 12 | 6 | Chief Product Officer | Unit-cost budgets; task-specific routing; caching; deterministic processing; usage controls; cost dashboards. | Cost per company refresh, answer, or report exceeds its approved budget for two periods. | Apply quotas, route to lower-cost workflows, disable nonessential generation, and revisit packaging. | Identified / monthly after AI integration |

| PR-014 | Human review becomes a bottleneck or is bypassed, weakening safety while preventing scale. | 3 | 4 | 12 | 6 | Equity Research Lead | Risk-based review queues; clear reviewer roles; versioning and approval audit; confidence-based escalation. | Review backlog exceeds the approved service target or unapproved exports occur. | Restrict generation/export volume, prioritize high-impact items, and add reviewer capacity or narrower scope. | Identified / Phase 10 beta |
| PR-015 | Enterprise buyers reject the product because audit, security, retention, administration, or deployment expectations are incomplete. | 3 | 5 | 15 | 5 | Enterprise Product Manager | Enterprise acceptance checklist; admin controls; auditability; retention configuration; security and DR evidence. | Beta procurement/security review produces a release-blocking gap. | Hold enterprise release, document remediation plan, and limit use to an approved pilot boundary. | Identified / Phase 11 readiness |
